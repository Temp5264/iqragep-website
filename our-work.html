Our Work

Bringing worldly and Islamic education to students who can’t access or afford it.

Where we work

  • Indonesia — Balikpapan, and Sumatra, where a school roof needs funding.
  • Nepal — Jamia Aaesha Siddiqah / Iqra Educational Academy, Ramaul, Siraha.
  • Bangladesh
  • Pakistan

Work in Africa and the USA is in progress.

What we do

We support schools and students who cannot access or afford education — helping with school buildings and facilities, learning materials, and both worldly and Islamic teaching, so children can learn, grow, and lead.
